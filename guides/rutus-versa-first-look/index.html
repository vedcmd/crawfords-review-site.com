Rutus Versa First Look: The Polish Multi-Frequency Wildcard

The Rutus Versa lands at Crawfords — five multi-frequency modes or a tunable 4–40 kHz single frequency, waterproof to 2.5m, WiFi updates. First look, before the field test.

Published 28 November 2026 · By the Crawfords Metal Detectors team

A new name has arrived on the Crawfords shelves: the Rutus Versa, from the small Polish manufacturer Rutus. This is a first look based on the specification and our counter-side handling — it has not yet been through our field-test process, so there is no editorial score here. We would rather tell you that plainly than dress a spec read-through up as a verdict.

What makes it interesting

The headline is frequency flexibility that nothing else at its price matches: five multi-frequency modes, or a single frequency you can tune anywhere from 4 to 40 kHz — in 0.2 kHz steps up to 14.2 kHz and 0.6 kHz steps above that. An EMI preview helps you dodge electrical interference. To be clear on the badge question: this is Rutus's own multi-frequency system, not Minelab Multi-IQ — our frequency technology guide explains why the platforms differ.

The spec that matters

Waterproof to 2.5 metres — wading and wet sand, not diving. Internal rechargeable battery quoted at 10–25 hours (about 15 typical), chargeable from a power bank while you detect, which is a genuinely practical touch. Seven factory search programs (including Beach and Beach Dual), four user slots, a 120-point target ID scale running -29 to 90, automatic ground balance with a Hot Rock setting, and software updates over WiFi. It weighs 1,330 g with the DD28 coil and folds to 60 cm. Warranty is 2 years.

Where it might fit

On paper the Versa squares up against the Nokta Legend 2 — another value-focused, fully-waterproof multi-frequency machine with deep customisation. The Nokta is submersible further and has a bigger UK user base; the Rutus counters with that tunable single-frequency range, which tinkerers will love. Expect a learning period: 30 sensitivity levels and 120-point notch control is not a switch-on-and-go proposition.

Our honest position

We stock it, it is in store now, and we are genuinely curious — but curiosity is not a score. The Versa goes onto the leaderboard only after it has done proper time in Lincolnshire soil. Until then, the full specification and current price are on the Rutus Versa product page, and the team on 01724 845608 will give you a straight answer on whether it suits your ground.
